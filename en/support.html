Frequently asked questions

How do I join a session?

On the Explore screen or on the map, open a session and tap I want to join. If you are asked to sign in, you can use Sign in with Apple or the code sent to your email. You can add a short note. When the host approves your request you get a notification and the session chat opens. In organizer sessions approval can be automatic.

When does a session chat open and close?

The chat is only for approved participants and the host. It becomes read-only 24 hours after the session ends, and it is deleted 7 days after the session ends.

How do I report a session, a message or a user?

Report a session with the Report session button on the session screen. Report a message by long-pressing it. Report a user or a profile photo from that person's profile. Reports are reviewed by the SeansUp team within 24 hours. If needed, content is removed or the account is suspended.

How do I block someone?

Use Block on the user's profile. Sessions by a blocked user are hidden from you, their join requests are refused and their messages are not shown to you. The block works in both directions. You can manage your blocks in Settings.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account and complete the confirmation steps. Your profile, photo, session memberships, join requests, blocks, the sessions you host and their chats are deleted, and your sign-in account is removed. If you signed in with Apple, the Apple connection is revoked as well. Messages you wrote in chats remain, shown as "Deleted user". Reports you filed are kept without your name as a moderation record. Deletion cannot be undone.

Why does the app ask for my location? What if I say no?

Location is used only to sort sessions by distance from you. It is used on your device and is not stored on our servers; the distance shown in Explore is computed on your device. The one exception is "Search place": the text you type and, if you allowed it, your approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) are forwarded to Apple Maps through our server to suggest places near you; we do not store these searches. If you say no, you can keep using the app by choosing a city.

Why do you ask for my gender?

Only to check access to women-only sessions. Your gender is not shown to other users or on your profile. It is set once in the app and cannot be changed there afterwards. If you think it was set in error, write to ismailcankaratasss@gmail.com.

I did not receive my sign-in code.

Check your spam or junk folder and wait a few minutes. If it still does not arrive, write to ismailcankaratasss@gmail.com.

How do I access, correct or delete my data?

You can edit your profile and delete your account in the app. For access, correction, deletion or objection requests, write to ismailcankaratasss@gmail.com. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I get the organizer badge?

The verified organizer badge is granted manually by SeansUp. There is no in-app application. If you are interested, write to ismailcankaratasss@gmail.com.

Is SeansUp a gym or a coach?

No. SeansUp helps you find workout sessions opened by users and organizers. We do not give training, health or medical advice.
