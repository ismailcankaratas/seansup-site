Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy explains how personal data is handled when you use the SeansUp app and this website.

1. Data controller

SeansUp is operated by the individual developer İsmail Can Karataş, who is the data controller of your personal data. For questions and requests: ismailcankaratasss@gmail.com

2. What data we collect

  • Account: your email address (or the email or private relay address Apple gives you) and a user ID.
  • Profile: your name, one optional profile photo, sports, level, training goal (up to 120 characters) and the visibility setting of your past sessions.
  • Gender (female / male / prefer not to say): your own statement. It is used only to verify access to women-only sessions. It is not shown on your profile or to any other user. It is set once and cannot be changed in the app afterwards.
  • Session data: sessions you host, meeting points you suggest (name, location and district; shown to other users only as an approximate area until approved), join requests and their status, "showed up" marks (used to compute your show-up rate), messages in session chats, the users you block and the reports you file.
  • Push token: only if you allow notifications. It is a technical code that identifies your device to the notification service. It is deleted when you sign out, when you turn notifications off in Settings, and when you delete your account. Notifications are sent only for join requests, request approvals and a reminder 1 hour before a session; they never contain message text, only the sport, the time and (for a join request) the first word of the requester's name.
  • Location: only if you allow it. See section 4.

To pick a profile photo, iOS's system photo picker is used. The app does not request access to your photo library or camera.

3. Why we use it

  • To show sessions, and to run join requests and session chats.
  • To let you sign in to your account (including email sign-in codes).
  • To keep people safe: handling reports, enforcing blocks and preventing abuse.
  • To send notifications, if you allowed them.
  • To check access to women-only sessions, and for no other purpose.

4. Location

Location permission is requested only while you are using the app, and it is optional. If you allow it, your location is used on your device to sort and filter sessions by distance from you. Your location is not stored on our servers. The one exception is place search: the text you type in "Search place" and, if you allowed it, your approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) are forwarded to Apple Maps through our server function to suggest places near you; we do not store these searches. If you do not allow it, you can keep using the app by choosing a city.

Session locations (a gym or a public meeting point) are visible to everyone. We do not ask for or publish home addresses.

5. No tracking or advertising

We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs. We do not share your data for advertising and we do not sell it. The app does not ask for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency).

6. Who can see what

  • Another user's profile can be opened only from within a session (session details, the participant list or a chat). It shows: their name, photo, sports, goal, show-up rate, the number of sessions they hosted and joined, and (if they have enabled it) the sport, venue and date of their last 10 past sessions, without times.
  • Your upcoming sessions and women-only sessions are not shown to other users.
  • A session chat is visible only to that session's approved participants and its host.
  • If you block someone, or someone blocks you, you cannot see each other's sessions, messages or profiles.
  • Report records are reviewed for moderation purposes only, by the SeansUp team.

7. Service providers

We use the following providers only to run the service. We do not use advertising networks, analytics tools or data buyers.

  • Supabase: database, authentication, profile photo storage and server functions. Data is hosted in the European region (Ireland).
  • Resend: sends transactional emails, including sign-in codes.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple. You can open a session's meeting point in Apple Maps. When you search for a place, the text you type and your approximate location are sent to Apple Maps servers (Apple Maps Server API). If you delete your account, the Apple connection is revoked.
  • Expo push service and Apple Push Notification service (APNs): deliver notifications, if you allow them. Your push token and the notification text pass through these services.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Session chats: become read-only 24 hours after the session ends and are deleted 7 days after the session ends.
  • Deleting your account: in the app, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account. Your profile, photo, gender and sports/goal details, session memberships, join requests, blocks, push tokens, the sessions you host and their chats, and your sign-in account are deleted. If you signed in with Apple, the Apple connection is revoked as well.
  • What stays anonymous: messages you wrote in chats remain, shown as "Deleted user". Reports you filed are kept without your name as a moderation record.

Account deletion cannot be undone.

9. Your rights

Under Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law no. 6698 (KVKK) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

  • learn whether your personal data is processed and request information about it,
  • access your data,
  • have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected,
  • have your data deleted or destroyed,
  • object to the processing of your data.

You can edit your profile and delete your account in the app. For other requests, write to ismailcankaratasss@gmail.com. We may ask you for information needed to verify your identity. If your request is refused or you are not satisfied with the answer, you may lodge a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu).

10. Children

SeansUp is not intended for anyone under 18. By signing up you confirm that you are at least 18.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always published on this page, and the date above changes when it does. For important changes we may also inform you in the app.
