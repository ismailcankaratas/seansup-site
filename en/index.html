Find the session, not the person.

SeansUp shows you where and when a workout is happening today. A gym, a seafront run, a park or a court: send a request to join a session, the host approves, and you train together.

What SeansUp is not

SeansUp is not a gym, a coach or a dating app. There is no list of people, no likes and no private messaging. A chat exists only inside a session, for its approved participants.
