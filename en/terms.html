Terms of Use

Last updated: 29 October 2026

These terms apply when you use the SeansUp app. By using the app you accept them.

Zero tolerance: we have zero tolerance for harassment, threats, hate speech, sexual content, fake profiles, fraud and sharing other people's personal information without consent. Content that breaks these rules is removed, and accounts may be suspended or deleted without prior warning.

1. The service

SeansUp helps you find workout sessions at a specific place and time, and send requests to join them. A session's chat is open only to its approved participants and its host. Sessions are created by users and organizers. SeansUp is not a gym, a coach or a session organizer. The service is currently offered in Istanbul, Turkey.

2. Minimum age

You must be at least 18 years old to use the app. By signing up you confirm this.

3. Your account

You must provide accurate information. You are responsible for keeping your account secure. You can delete your account at any time in Profile → Settings → Delete account. Deletion cannot be undone, and what is deleted or kept is described in the Privacy Policy.

4. Prohibited conduct and content

The following are not allowed:

  • Harassment, threats, hate speech, insults or discrimination.
  • Sexual content or sexually motivated behaviour.
  • Fake profiles, fake sessions or impersonation.
  • Fraud, fake payment or sales requests.
  • Sharing another person's personal information, photos or messages without consent.
  • Contact for purposes other than sessions: dating, sales or advertising.

5. Reports, blocking and moderation

  • You can report sessions, messages, users and profile photos from within the app.
  • You can block users you do not want to interact with. A blocked user's sessions are hidden from you, their requests are refused and their messages are not shown to you.
  • We commit to reviewing reports and acting on them within 24 hours.
  • We may remove content that breaks these terms, and suspend or delete accounts. We may do this without prior warning.

6. Safety and liability

Sessions take place in person, with real people. When you meet:

  • Meet in public places.
  • Tell someone you trust when needed.
  • In an emergency, call 112.

You are responsible for being physically fit to exercise. SeansUp does not give medical, health or training advice.

To the extent permitted by law, SeansUp is not liable for meetings, accidents or disputes between session hosts and participants.

7. Women-only sessions

Gender is used only to verify access to women-only sessions. You must answer truthfully. A false statement can lead to your account being closed.

8. Organizer badge

The verified organizer badge is granted manually by SeansUp. There is no in-app application for it.

9. Your content

You are responsible for the name, goal, descriptions, sessions and messages you write. You allow us to process this content to provide the service, and to show it to other users.
